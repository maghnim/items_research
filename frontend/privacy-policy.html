Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Pricera ("Pricera", "we", "us", or "our") provides an automated competitor price-tracking service (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and a securely hashed password.

Billing information. When you subscribe to a paid plan, our payment processors (Stripe and PayPal) collect and process your payment card or PayPal account details directly. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.

Product tracking data. We store the URLs you submit for tracking, along with the prices, stock status, and timestamps our system collects from those public pages.

Usage data. We automatically collect limited technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, to maintain security and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including scheduled price checks and alert delivery.
  • To process subscription payments and manage your billing status.
  • To send you transactional emails, including price-change alerts and account notices.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse of the Service.
  • To communicate with you about updates to the Service or this policy.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal) to process subscription payments.
  • Infrastructure providers (hosting, database, and email delivery providers) who process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal authorities where required to comply with applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

4. Data Retention

We retain account and price-history data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal account information within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal, tax, or billing-dispute purposes.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can manage most account data directly from your dashboard, or contact us at the address below to make a request.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage to keep you logged in and remember your session. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including password hashing and encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by email or through the Service.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@pricera.online or via our contact page.

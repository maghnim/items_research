About PricePilot

Built so sellers stop checking competitor prices by hand

PricePilot started as a simple question: why are online sellers still opening ten browser tabs every morning just to see if a competitor changed a price?

We build tools for independent sellers, small ecommerce teams, and agencies who compete on price and can't afford to be the last to notice when the market moves. PricePilot automates the tedious part — watching pages, comparing prices, remembering history — so you can focus on the decision, not the checking.

Our monitoring engine understands the major ecommerce platforms out of the box — Shopify, WooCommerce, and Amazon listings — and falls back to configurable detection for everything else. Every price point we collect is stored, so you get a full history, not just a snapshot.

We take the legal and ethical side of monitoring seriously: PricePilot only tracks publicly visible product pages, applies rate limiting to avoid burdening the sites we monitor, and gives you full control over which pages you track.

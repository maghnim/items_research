Refund Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

We want you to be confident in your PricePilot subscription. This policy explains how refunds and cancellations work.

1. Free Trial

Every new subscription starts with a 14-day free trial. No payment is collected during the trial, and you can cancel at any time before it ends at no cost.

2. Monthly Subscriptions

Paid plans are billed monthly in advance. Because the Service provides continuous monitoring throughout each billing period, payments for the current billing period are generally non-refundable once the period has begun.

3. Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time from the billing portal in your dashboard. When you cancel:

  • You retain access to your current plan until the end of the billing period you've already paid for.
  • You will not be charged again after cancellation.
  • No partial refund is issued for the unused portion of the current billing period, except as described below.

4. Exceptions

We will consider a full or partial refund on a case-by-case basis if:

  • You were charged due to a verified billing error or duplicate charge.
  • The Service was unavailable or materially non-functional for a significant portion of your billing period due to an issue on our end.
  • You were charged after requesting cancellation before the renewal date.

To request a refund under these circumstances, contact us within 14 days of the charge at billing@pricepilot.app with your account email and the reason for the request. We aim to respond within 3 business days.

5. Plan Changes

If you upgrade your plan, the new rate applies immediately and is prorated for the remainder of the current billing cycle. If you downgrade, the new rate takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

6. Payment Method

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method used (Stripe for card payments, or PayPal for PayPal subscriptions) and may take 5–10 business days to appear depending on your bank or PayPal processing times.

7. Contact Us

For any billing or refund question, reach us at billing@pricepilot.app or via our contact page.
